Vocabsolution — a complete vocabulary solution for everyone.

The full content of the book "VOCABSOLUTION by English Doctor" by Shahjalal Sultanate (English Doctor), built into an app for Bengali-speaking learners. Its idea: understand, don't memorise. Break words into parts, learn whole word families from one root, and remember hard words through short Bengali stories. Explanations and meanings are in Bengali.

Who it is for
• Candidates for BCS, bank, NTRCA and primary-teacher recruitment exams in Bangladesh
• IELTS candidates aiming for a higher band
• Anyone who wants to read, write and speak English with confidence

All 11 chapters of the book
1. Word Building Basics: 48 suffixes, 48 prefixes, word-breaking practice
2. Root Power: 96 word families, 2,694 words
3. Memory Hooks: 100 hard words with Bengali stories and sound hooks
4. Commonly Confused Words: 100 pairs
5. Word Equivalents: synonyms, antonyms and one-word substitutions
6. Everyday Collocations: 200 collocations and common mistakes
7. Listen Smart: IELTS Listening words, spelling traps, a listen-and-spell game
8. Read Smart: 150 passages with 1,500 academic words in context
9. The Paraphrase Bank: 1,152 collocations with paraphrases
10. Write Smart: high-band language for Task 1 and Task 2
11. Exam Ready: 250 job-exam MCQs in 10 sets with a 20-minute timer

How you learn
• The book's 1-3-7-30 day repetition method: the app tells you which words to review and when
• A placement test at the start, with advice on where to begin
• Quizzes and games in every chapter
• Parts of speech marked by colour and symbol (■ noun ▲ verb ● adjective ◆ adverb)
• British pronunciation and IPA

Also included
• Daily goal, streak, XP and badges
• "Word of the Day" home-screen widget
• Share a word or story as an image card
• Search in English or Bengali, bookmarks, personal notes
• Daily reminder, dark mode, adjustable text size

Works offline, no ads
All content is on your phone. The app does not collect or send any personal data. Your progress stays on your device (and in your own Google backup if device backup is on).
