Privacy Policy

Effective: 12 November 2026

English Doctor BD ("we") runs this website and teaches IELTS, PTE and Spoken English courses. This policy explains what information we handle and why.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. When you use the contact form, it opens WhatsApp or your email app with your name, email, phone, subject and message filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send there, and this website itself does not store the form.
  • Admission details. When you enrol, we keep the details needed to run your course: name, phone, email, course, batch, payment records and your mock-test results and feedback.
  • No tracking. This website uses no analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of serving the font.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and arrange admission.
  • To run classes, mock tests and feedback, and to track your progress.
  • To send important course updates (class times, changes). We do not send marketing messages without your consent.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. For study abroad services we share documents with universities, scholarship bodies or visa authorities only when you ask us to apply on your behalf. Messages you send through WhatsApp or email are also subject to those services' own policies.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep student records while you are enrolled and for a reasonable period afterwards for certificates and references. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing englishdoctorbd@gmail.com.

Vocabsolution app

The Vocabsolution Android app has its own policy: it collects no personal data and keeps your progress only on your phone.

Children

Students under 18 should enrol with the knowledge of a parent or guardian, who may contact us about the student's information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date.
