FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which course should I start with?

New to IELTS? Start with Basic to IELTS. Already comfortable with English? Choose IELTS Focus, or IELTS Crash if your exam is close. Not sure — take the free mock test first.

Are mock tests included?

Yes. IELTS courses include regular Cambridge-style mock tests with detailed feedback.

Do you help with study abroad and visas?

Yes — counseling, university applications, scholarships and visa support for the UK, Canada, Australia, USA and Europe.

How do I pay?

bKash, Nagad, Rocket or card. Contact us about instalments.
